# Bayt: Product Brief

Bayt is a real estate search app that gives buyers clarity before they purchase.

## Goals

1. **Clarity before purchase.**
   Give buyers peace of mind about what they are buying.
2. **Real estate search you can understand.**
3. **Empower the buyer.**

## Users

1. Real estate investors
2. Home buyers
3. Home inspectors
4. Agents
5. The city

## Features

### 1. Custom investment-strategy-based search

Users describe their strategy in plain language, and Bayt finds properties that fit it.
Example queries:

- "Looking for property with 6-8% return."
- "Here is my income."
- "Might want to turn a 2-bed into a 3-bed."

To answer these, Bayt pulls together:

1. **Floor plans.**
   Use existing plans when available, or generate them from listing photos.
2. **MLS data.**
3. **Zoning laws and city requirements.**
4. **Contractor estimate and material estimate.**
