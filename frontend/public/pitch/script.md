# Bayt pitch video: narration script

Voice: Kokoro-82M `am_michael`, speed 1.12, generated locally with `hyperframes tts`. One clip per scene; each scene lasts as long as its clip plus a short beat.

| # | Scene | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cold open, slogan | Search by taste, not by filters. This is Bayt. |
| 2 | The problem | Every search site gives you price, beds and baths. Nobody shops that way. Ask a realtor what they hear most: "I want a home similar to this one." Filters cannot answer that. Bayt reads every listing the way a buyer would, finds the homes you would love, and says why. |
| 3 | Live search as an investor | Bayt is live with two thousand two hundred sixty-one Toronto listings. Say who you are: an investor. Sort by best fit. Every tile now carries a score for that buyer, because a model has already read each house. |
| 4 | Taste Match diagram | The engine is Taste Match. Like the Netflix recommender, a language model reads what each thing is about. Nemotron three point five Super V L reads the text, eight photos and the lot data, and writes a House Profile: a pitch and a score for four buyers. The pitches become vectors. Open a house, the thirty nearest light up, and Nemotron ranks the best eight with a reason each. Say "like this, but a garage", and the list moves with you. |
| 5 | Live More like this and steering | Thirty-nine Rhydwen Avenue: one house, four scores, four reasons. Press More like this. Eight homes ranked for an investor, each with a reason in plain words. Now steer: a bigger backyard and a garage. Every reason answers the ask. |
| 6 | Taste Profile and saves | Save a few homes and Bayt builds a Taste Profile for your session, no login. After five actions Nemotron sums up your taste, guesses your buyer type, and makes it your default. |
| 7 | Precedent Check diagram | Builders and investors need precedent. Precedent Check reads every Committee of Adjustment file since two thousand one within five hundred metres. Around thirty-nine Rhydwen: two hundred twenty-two files, one hundred eighty-eight approved, nine refused. The city publishes decisions, not variance tables, so an approval nearby is precedent, not a promise. Build Check takes your plan, finds the gaps against By-law five sixty-nine, lists the routes and risks, and Nemotron three Super writes the answer, citing the clauses and the nearby files. |
| 8 | Live lot, committee, Build Check, Floor Sketch | Lot and Zoning draws the parcel to scale. Open the committee card and the table is there. Type a plan into Build Check and the cited answer arrives in seconds. Floor Sketch fits the listed rooms into the building outline. |
| 9 | Models | Every call runs on Nebius Token Factory through its OpenAI-style API. Nemotron three point five Super V L reads eight photos in about six seconds, and profiled the whole catalogue in seventy-eight minutes for about eight dollars. Nemotron three Super, a one hundred twenty billion parameter mixture of experts, reasons over long by-law text cheaply, with thinking off for fast structured answers. Qwen three Embedding gives the vectors. Postgres holds it all. |
| 10 | End card | Bayt. Search by taste. Live at bayt dot andalsolutions dot com, open source on GitHub. |

About 400 spoken words, under 3 minutes with the beats between scenes, as Devpost requires. The on-screen lower-thirds are shorter than the narration and never repeat it word for word.
